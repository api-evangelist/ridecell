# Ridecell

> Ridecell is an AI-powered asset intelligence and orchestration platform for automotive leasing, fleet, and finance companies. Its intelligence modules and the "Maya" AI agent monitor fleets, detect issues, and automate operational decisions across delivery, spend, supplier, asset, and operational domains — processing 12M+ events daily across 500K+ assets and 100+ connected systems.
